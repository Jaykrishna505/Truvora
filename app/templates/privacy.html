Privacy Policy

How Truvora handles platform data

Truvora stores business account details, review request records, private feedback, delivery logs, payment status, and local Google review button click tracking.

Google review click tracking records that a person clicked the Google review button. It does not confirm whether they submitted a Google review or what they posted on Google.

Businesses should only upload contact information when they have a lawful reason to contact that person. Production deployments should use secure hosting, HTTPS, access controls, and verified email/SMS providers.

This starter privacy policy should be reviewed by a qualified attorney before public launch.
